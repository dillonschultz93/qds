Add the Tier 3 components, which fill the roadmap's budget of 30:

- `qds-combobox`, a text field with a list of options that narrows as you type, following the WAI-ARIA combobox pattern.
- `qds-popover`, a non-modal panel of content opened by a button, beside it and above the page.
- `qds-skeleton`, a placeholder shape shown where content is loading.
- `qds-slider`, a labeled range input that submits with its form.
- `qds-file-upload`, a place to choose files by browsing or dropping them, with the chosen files listed and removable.
- `qds-segmented-control` and `qds-segment`, a compact row of mutually exclusive options.

The built-in icons gain `file` and `upload`.
